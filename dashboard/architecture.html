Sampling is stochastic (top-k 50, temperature 1.0 defaults), so the same text gives a slightly different read each run. The Base model has no emotion or style input: delivery comes from the reference clip plus the meaning of the text.

Stage by stage: input, output, method, weak spots

StageInput → outputMethod / modelWeak spots and what to ideate on
CaptureYour voice → 32-bit float 48 kHz WAVBrowser mic with all processing off; AudioWorklet PCMResult depends on your room and mic. Idea: guided mic check (level, room tone, reverb estimate) before recording.
IngestUpload → mono 48 kHz float + quality badgesffmpeg; peak, clipping, noise floor, SNR statisticsNo reverb or sibilance check yet. Idea: reject or warn on room echo, plosives, clipping.
Reference pickPart A → one ~15 s clip + exact transcriptWhisper small timestamps; heuristic window scoringHeuristic, not learned: it can't tell flat from expressive reading. The reference is the single biggest lever on cloned voice and delivery. Ideas: score candidates by speaker similarity and prosody variety; try several clips and A/B; a bank of per-emotion references from Part B.
Text prepText → chunks of up to 280 chars with fixed gapsRegex sentence splitterFixed gaps may not match natural rhythm. Idea: punctuation-aware gaps, or generate longer passages in one pass and compare, or stitch with crossfades.
Synthesisref + text → 24 kHz speechQwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base: speaker embedding + in-context reference tokens; talker + code predictor produce 16-codebook RVQ tokens at 12.5 Hz; decoder to audioSlow (about 4x slower than real time here). Quantised 12.5 Hz tokens cap fine detail and top-end. No style control. Ideas: Qwen3-TTS 0.6B for speed, or other models for quality; sampling temperature and top-k tuning; pick the best of N takes automatically; fine-tune on your voice (LoRA) instead of one-shot cloning.
Master: tonal24 kHz → 48 kHz, filtered, de-essed, EQ'dPolyphase resample; zero-phase Butterworth; band-split compressor for de-essing; shelving and peaking EQThe EQ is a fixed guess, not matched to your voice. Idea: match EQ to your own recording's spectrum, or to a studio reference.
Master: dynamicsEQ'd audio → compressed, 3 dB average gain reduction3-band compressor, then bus compressor with threshold chosen by bisectionCompression follows the target of 3 dB, but TTS audio is already fairly even. Ideas: make it optional, or measure whether it helps listener preference.
Master: loudness→ -16 LUFS, -1 dBTPBS.1770 integrated loudness; lookahead limiter; 4x oversampled true-peak checkSolid and standard. Target could be tuned per platform (e.g. -14 for streaming, -19 for podcasts).

Installed but not in the live path

  • IndexTTS-2.5 (emotion vectors + duration control): works on the GPU, replaced by Qwen for the current test.
  • Qwen3-4B-Instruct tagger: LLM that split text into emotion and pace segments. Unused because Qwen3-TTS-Base can't take tags.
  • Spectral-gate cleaner (noisereduce): removed from the profile step as too invasive.
  • DeepFilterNet: failed to install on this machine.

Measured so far

  • Qwen3-TTS 1.7B: 5.6 GB peak VRAM, about 4.4 s compute per second of audio (first runs, includes warm-up).
  • Mastering: about 4 s per job; lands at -16.0 LUFS, about -1.2 dBTP, about 3.0 dB gain reduction.
  • One job (two short paragraphs): about 85-90 s end to end.

Ideas to explore next (untested)

Naturalness and pacing
  • Compare chunk size: sentence vs paragraph vs whole text
  • Punctuation-aware pauses instead of fixed gaps
  • Generate N takes per chunk, auto-pick by a quality score
  • Different reference clips, A/B them blind
Emotion and style
  • Reference bank from your Part B lines (excited, soft, serious, sad...), picked per paragraph
  • LLM chooses the reference per sentence from your style description
  • Qwen3-TTS CustomVoice / VoiceDesign for instruct control (does not clone you directly)
Fidelity
  • Fine-tune (LoRA) on your 2-minute recording or a longer one
  • Bandwidth extension above 12 kHz
  • Match mastering EQ to your recorded voice
Measurement
  • Speaker-similarity score (embedding cosine) vs your real recording
  • Whisper word-error rate on outputs to catch skipped or mispronounced words
  • Automated MOS-style naturalness predictor, plus your own listening notes per job
Speed
  • Qwen3-TTS 0.6B for drafts, 1.7B for finals
  • Keep the model loaded between jobs (saves load time)
  • Test whether disabling MIOpen workaround or other ROCm settings speeds generation
