3 · Options & generate

lower = more varied delivery, higher = closer to the reference

 







 

  
1 · Speaker & recording

  

   Quiet room · 10–15 cm from mic · speak naturally. Mic processing (echo cancel/noise suppress/AGC) is switched off for you.

  

 

 

  
2 · Reference clip

  

   Takes the best ~15 s sentence from Part A, untouched, and transcribes it. Used for plain generation.

  

 

 

  
3 · Intonation clips

  

   Cuts one clip per labelled delivery from every part you recorded (neutral, emotions, question shapes, conversation, explainer, story, ads, pacing, emphasis, numbers, long form), measures each clip's cadence, and packs everything into one .vcref. Needs parts A, B and C; more parts means more clips. Takes a few minutes.
