# Enrollment recording (~2 minutes total)

## Setup
- Quiet, soft room (closet or bedroom with clothes/bedding beats an empty room). Phone/PC fans off.
- Same mic you'll want to be cloned on. Mouth 10-15 cm from the mic, off-axis slightly, pop filter if you have one.
- Record 48 kHz (or 44.1), 24-bit WAV, mono. No noise reduction, no EQ, no auto-gain. Peaks around -12 dB, never clipping.
- Leave 2 s of silence at the very start (room tone, used for noise profiling).
- Read naturally, like talking to one person. Don't perform; small mistakes are fine, just repeat the sentence and keep going.
- Save each part as its own file: reference/<name>/A_neutral.wav, B_expressive.wav, C_short.wav

## Part A - Neutral narration (~60 s)
Warm, steady, conversational. Normal pace.

> The big brown fox jumped quickly over the fence, then paused beside a quiet stream.
> Thirty-three thieves thought they thrilled the throne throughout Thursday.
> She sells seashells by the shore, and the measure of pleasure is a rare treasure.
> Judge George chose a large orange from the church's garden.
> "Why would you ask?" Yvonne said. "We all know the answer, don't we?"
> A cheerful young man could hardly wait to bring home a loaf of fresh bread, a few green beans, and some very ripe peaches.
> The pale moon rose over the vast, calm lake; a gentle breeze moved through the tall pines, and far off, a lone bird called.
> Please call Paul at five-oh-nine, eight-seven-six, four-three-two-one, or send it by noon on June twenty-ninth.
> Dad took the book out of the rug-covered box; Ken's boat rode the foam toward home.

## Part B - Expressive (~35 s)
Do each line fully in the stated emotion. Commit to it; this is what drives emotional range.

- (Excited, fast) "No way! You actually got it done? That is absolutely incredible, I knew you could do it!"
- (Warm, soft, slow) "Take your time. There's no rush at all. Everything is going to be just fine."
- (Serious, low, deliberate) "Listen carefully. What happens next matters more than anything we've done so far."
- (Sad, quiet) "I really thought it would be different this time. I miss how things used to be."
- (Annoyed, sharp) "Seriously? Again? I told you twice already. Just put it back where it belongs."
- (Playful, teasing) "Oh, you think you're so clever, don't you? Well, we'll just see about that."

## Part C - Short utterances and questions (~15 s)
Pause about a second between each.

> Yes. / No. / Maybe. / Right. / Okay, sure. / Hmm, let me think. / Really? / What do you mean? / Wait, hold on. / Thank you so much. / Ha, that's great. / Ahh, finally.
